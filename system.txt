Information for Students and Applicants:
- Admission Requirements: Applicants need a standard set of documents: high school diploma (аттестат), UNT (ЕНТ/ҰБТ) certificate, and several 3x4 photos.
- Educational Grants: To secure a grant, applicants must achieve a passing score and *must explicitly participate* in the grant competition. Scoring high is not enough; submitting the application for the competition is key.
- IT & Platonus Support: For all inquiries regarding university IT systems, usernames, and passwords for Platonus, visit Room 249 in Academic Building #1.

Degree Levels:
- Bachelor's Degree (Бакалавриат)
- Master's Degree (Магистратура)
- Doctoral Degree / PhD (Докторантура)

Anti-Plagiarism System:
All written academic works (courseworks, theses, essays, research papers, dissertations) by students and faculty members undergo plagiarism checks via https://www.strikeplagiarism.com to ensure academic integrity.
Rules and thresholds are published on the website: https://asu.edu.kz/university/ruler/Vice-Rector-for-Academic-Affairs/18610/
- Document submission: accepted formats are DOC, DOCX, PDF. Must include a title page, author's full name, and topic.
- Verification base: public internet sources, academic journals/books/dissertations database, and the university’s internal repository.
- Checked by dedicated operators assigned to each department.

Microsoft Teams Guidelines:
- Access via Platonus: Log into Platonus -> Personal Account -> Study Schedule (Calendar) -> Click the Teams link next to the class name. The desktop/mobile app should be pre-installed; otherwise, it opens in a browser (app is recommended).
- Download Links: Desktop via official website https://www.microsoft.com/teams. Mobile apps via Google Play (Android) or App Store (iOS) by searching "Microsoft Teams". Use university-provided credentials to log in.
- Support: Contact the Vice Dean for Academic Affairs or visit Academic Building #1, Room 249.
- Logins & Passwords: New students/faculty receive a login and a temporary password (must be changed upon first login). Issued by the ICT Development Department (Building 1, Room 249) or advisors/deans.
- Email formats: e.g., a.ivanov@asu.edu.kz or aidana.bekova@asu.edu.kz.

Certificates, Transcripts, and Transfers:
- Student Enrollment Certificate: Standard certificate for schools, banks, public transport can be obtained in Room 249.
- Online Certificates: Other certificates can be requested online via Platonus. They are digitally signed by the Rector, contain a QR code, and are legally equivalent to paper documents under RK Law No.370-II dated Jan 7, 2003 (Art. 7, Clause 1).
- Social Applications: Appx №2 & №4 (loss of provider allowance), Appx №6 (disability/age allowance), Appx №29 (large families), Appx №31 (GFSS payments). Note: Consult your local PSC (ЦОН) to verify which appendix you need.
- Military Appendix (Appx №3): For conscript-aged students, issued only after registering with the university’s military department.
- Video tutorial on certificates: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIZkCPqhhrI
- Transcript: Requested online via the "Application" section in Platonus. Available in Kazakh, Russian, and English with QR code validation.
- University Transfer: To transfer to another university, students must fill out the "Application for transfer to another university" in Platonus using their digital signature (ЭЦП). Status can be tracked under "Clearance Sheet" (Келісу парағы). Any academic or financial debts will be listed there. Final documents are downloaded under "Issued Documents".
